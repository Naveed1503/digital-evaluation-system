# Digital Evaluation System

Problem Statement

Traditional examination evaluation processes are often time-consuming, paper-intensive, and prone to human errors, inconsistencies, and delays. Managing answer scripts, evaluator allocation, marks entry, moderation, and result preparation manually creates significant administrative challenges. A Digital Evaluation System is required to automate and streamline the complete evaluation process. The proposed system enables secure digital answer-script access, evaluator assignment, online marking, automated tabulation, moderation, and result generation, thereby improving accuracy, transparency, efficiency, confidentiality, and timely declaration of examination results.

Key Objective

The objective is to develop a secure and efficient Digital Evaluation System that automates answer-script distribution, evaluator assignment, online valuation, marks entry, moderation, and result generation, thereby improving the accuracy, transparency, confidentiality, and timely completion of the examination evaluation process.
